Requires the relay firmware (app_version 99.x) on both boards and a Web Bluetooth browser (desktop or Android Chrome/Edge — not iOS Safari). BLE pairing is disabled on the Ultra by default, so no passkey is needed. The ghost always emulates once the mole has cloned the card.
Roles: Local = both boards on this browser. Mole/Ghost = two phones, each with one board, bridged through a relay server (same room code); the Ghost phone drives and the Mole phone serves. Latency adds one internet round-trip per APDU.
